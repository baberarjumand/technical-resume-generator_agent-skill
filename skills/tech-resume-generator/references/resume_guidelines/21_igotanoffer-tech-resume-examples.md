## 21.1 Seven golden rules (Cody + Candace)

1. **Answer the bar in 10 seconds.** Years of **role-relevant** experience (and years managing, if that’s the job) must be obvious. Cody: “Us recruiters are lazy.”
2. **Non-traditional header is allowed.** Cody puts **Career Summary** + **Impact Summary** bullets in the top third (`cody-nontraditional-header.png`): locations/remote, YoE, MGMT/EXEC counts, then trophy hires. Safe default for experienced candidates remains TIH headline + skills — not two recruiter-style summaries.
3. **No design.** Candace: pictures, **columns**, photos break ATS. No photo (discrimination + ATS).
4. **Locations / remote / relocate** in the header, e.g. `Locations: San Francisco | Remote | Hybrid within 30 miles of Bay Area`. For experienced candidates: city + **open to remote / relocate**, not a fake US city.
5. **Cut waffle.** “Experienced engineer passionate about making great products” is a fail. Years + products, or nothing.
6. **Numbers.** Downloads, days early, team size. “Words can tell a story but numbers tell it way better.”
7. **Skills / “technology snapshot”** for ATS keywords — don’t jam every sentence.

**Six “key skills” they want shown in bullets:** leadership (even as IC), communication / xfn, data (metrics), facilitation (unblock — managers), system design, engineering chops (PMs too). Not a Skills-line dump of “leadership.”

## 21.2 Their recommended skeleton vs what the samples actually do

**Karl / Google-experienced order:** Personal info → **Work** → Education → Leadership & Awards → Skills/Interests. Languages **in the header** (Google rec). Education first only if new-grad.

That **skills-last** order loses to Jachja / TIH / 99 (skills high). Their own **senior template** and Sam (Crunchyroll) put skills/languages near the top — follow those.

**Checklist:** 1 page unless experience “merits” 2; white space; langs listed; actions not duties; quantify; awards post-university if grad >10 years; PDF; proofread (including after each tweak); reverse-read for typos.

**Don’t:** street, photo, DOB, gender, labeled `email:`/`tel:`, summary/objective (Google: waste unless unusual profile), references, SAT/GMAT for experienced hires, high school after a degree, thesis unless recent, MS Word / “watching Netflix,” employee-of-the-week, school awards >10 years old.

**Dario (ex.4) structure (transcribed):** contact redacted → EXPERIENCE (Amazon SDM nested: Twitch Monetization / Buy With Prime / Alexa Smart Home Energy, then SDE titles 2012–2021, then 2009–2012 FAA work) → EDUCATION, CERTIFICATIONS & SKILLS (BCS 2009, AWS CSA, Six Sigma, then a pipe-separated AWS/Java/Kotlin dump). Scope sentence under current title. Example bullets: “Launched Preferred Deals… unlock 50% more ad inventory… 13 countries”; “conversion increase of 25%”; “core dev team from 1 FTE to 13… 90%+ manager satisfaction vs 50% company-wide”; “latency from ~6.5s at launch to ~0.5s at P99”; “cloud operating costs … more than 20% (vs a target of 15%)”; “300K to 2M [devices] in the first year and 122M today.”

Keep: nested titles, baselines, verb + metric + product. Drop: green ink, 5–7 bullets on one role, combined skills blob, certs that aren’t the candidate’s.

## 21.4 Google sibling + official How we hire

Yana: Google is data-driven; the CV should **think in metrics**, not just list outcomes.

Google-specific extras: **scrappiness** (side projects with scarce resources — their $80K gardening example is not a SWE model); **Googleyness** (ambiguity, bias for action, collaborative) — show in bullets, not a values dump; xfn + foreign languages as communication; Leadership & Awards section if you have real items.

**Official How we hire (fetched 10 Sep 2026):** job-specific résumé; align to **minimum qualifications**; XYZ; leadership = team size + scope; new-grad projects/coursework; “Keep it short. We don’t have a length requirement, but being clear and succinct are key.” Cover letters **not required**.

IGO A claims engineering résumés may run **2 or even 3** pages. Google’s own page says think twice before multiple pages. **99 stays one page.**

Google video: simple consistent font; Arial/Calibri 10–12; black and white; PDF; langs at top for SWE.

## 21.5 Senior SWE + keywords siblings

**Senior IC (not manager):** autonomy, scale, raise the team. Show: end-to-end initiatives; architecture across teams; standards others followed; messy trade-offs; owned production (on-call, incidents); mentorship / reviews / runbooks; product/business “why.” Depth in fewer languages > a tourist stack.

**Keywords page:** recruiters scan seconds; ATS wants **competency phrases** (“Debugged production issues affecting millions”). Lists are **verb starters** (Implemented…, Designed scalable systems, Mentored junior devs) — use only true ones. Spell `HTML5`, `SQL`, not `Html5`.

**GitHub (SWE sibling):** optional. Stale profile **hurts**. <5 YoE: a live profile helps. Else link **one** proud OSS repo. Don’t paste a dead GitHub.

**CAR** (Challenge–Action–Result) as a drafting twin of XYZ; still ship one dense bullet.
