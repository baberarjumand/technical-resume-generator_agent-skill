## 12.1 Hub writing rules

**Format**

- Font **10–12 pt**; margins **1" to 0.65"** (undergrad Word templates: **0.6"**, 10–10.5 pt to fit one page)
- Simple, consistent, white space, line breaks between sections
- Avoid stock templates, graphics, text boxes, Comic Sans, unprofessional fonts
- ATS often cannot read **tables, shapes, graphics, images**
- Submit **PDF named with your name** (`Jane Doe Resume`). Some portals still want copy-paste into a form — keep a plain version

**Length**

- Undergrads & recent grads: **one page**
- PhD/postdocs: **two+ pages OK**; most important content on page 1; **name + page number** on page 2 (header or footer)
- FAQ: résumé length follows years of experience — **1 page** undergrad/recent; **1–2 pages** professionals. Grad guide: **up to three pages** in some industries
- **Tension:** woodworksio/TIH hard 1-page for US SWE. Template note: 1 page “unless you are alum with advanced degree or **substantial work experience**.” For experienced industry SWE keep **one page**

**Layout**

- **Header:** name, email, phone. Street **not required**; City, State/Country OK. LinkedIn URL. GitHub/portfolio for tech
- **Education:** newest first, reverse chrono; degree, program, expected grad (e.g. June 20xx); optional courses, awards, thesis, GPA. **After you graduate the current program, move Education below Experience**
- **Work / Leadership:** generic “Experience” / “Leadership & Extracurriculars”, **or** tailored headers (“Research Experience”, “Marketing Experience”). Employee, intern, volunteer, researcher, teammate all count
- **Skills & Interests:** only **objective, measurable** skills (languages, software, lab, programming). Soft skills shown in bullets, not listed. Interests optional (personality)

**Impact bullets**

- Verb phrases; every word counts
- Avoid helping verbs: *assisted, worked with, helped, responsible for*
- Ask: skills used, numbers/scope, pride/accomplishments, tools, coworker praise, outcomes, improve/create/present/lead/initiate/solve/analyze
- **Actions + accomplishments:** not only that you did XX, but that you did XX **well** or with **YY results**, tied to the larger project

Hub examples (weak → strong):

- Helped with book research → “Completed review of **35** journal papers on inflammatory disease mechanisms and communicated summary of findings to professor **within 2 weeks**”
- Assisted in program planning → “Planned and implemented **4** neighborhood public outreach projects with a total attendance of **150** people…”
- Waited tables / handled complaints → “Managed the largest serving section, received positive feedback for remaining calm… fast resolutions…”

**Optional sections**

- **Summary / profile:** for PhDs and people further along who need a short tie-together across pages. Specific strengths only
- **Projects:** usually SWE/CS; GitHub in header too
- **Publications:** research jobs — bibliographic. Non-research: “Five publications in professional journals”
- **Honors:** with Education or separate; for unknown awards, stress competitiveness (“One of three selected from among 2,000…”)

**FAQ (hub)**

- Résumé vs CV: résumé tailored, short; CV academic full history (some countries use “CV” for everything)
- Multiple versions: yes, by field. Optional **long master résumé**, then cut per application (same as grad-guide Step 3)
- No field experience: transferable skills; internships expect training
- JD is a **wishlist** except federal jobs — apply if you meet **most**
- ATS: filters on keywords, skills, years. **Targeted Resume** and **JobScan** emulate (you may need to fix their keyword picks)
- Transfer / exchange / study abroad: Education, same format, **date range** not expected-grad; optional one descriptive bullet
- Preferred name OK (`Jane “Sam” Doe` / `Sam Doe`); legal name for background checks
- **SAT/ACT:** only finance/consulting, or if asked (matches Harvard/Yale student chrome — skip for an experienced candidate)
- **No references** on the résumé

## 12.2 Four undergraduate Word templates (8 Sep 2026)

One `.docx`, four letter pages, **no tables**. Fonts Arial / Calibri / Garamond. **0.6" all sides**. Degree-name tips for CAS / Wharton / SEAS / Nursing.

Shared instructional bullets: quantify (team of 6, $500 budget); no *worked with/helped/assisted*; show leadership with an example; skim for the main idea; you need not hit 100% of JD boxes; **1 page** unless alum + advanced degree or substantial work; **≥10 pt**, margins can be 0.6".

| # | Contact | After education | Rest |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 General | `email \| phone \| Philadelphia, PA` | Professional Experience | Leadership & Extracurriculars → Skills & Interests (Technical / Languages / Interests) |
| 2 LinkedIn / consulting | `email // phone // LinkedIn URL` | Relevant Experience | Leadership → Skills. Optional **SAT Math / R&W** under high school |
| 3 **CS / GitHub** | `email \| phone \| GitHub: URL` | **Technical Skills** (Programming; Platforms/Tools; optional Interests) | Relevant Experience → **Projects** → Leadership |
| 4 Research | `email \| phone \| Philadelphia, PA` | Research Experience | Leadership & Additional Experience (typo “EXPEREINCE”) → Skills (Lab / Technical / Interests). Optional senior thesis |

Template 3 is the SWE intern skeleton (education-first, skills after education, projects). **Do not use that order for an experienced candidate.** Contact pattern on #3 (GitHub, no street) is closer to TIH than the CS *filled* sample, which still uses a street address.

## 12.4 SCO method (grad 2-pager + 52-page guide)

**Skill – Context – Outcome** = Yale WHO / XYZ / TIH action→outcome.

2-pager contact: `City, State | Phone | Email | LinkedIn (optional) | portfolio (optional)` — **no street**.

- **Summary:** 3–5 **bullets** (not a paragraph), written **after** tailoring, each claim evidenced below. Optional on a 1-pager (grad guide: skip summary on short résumés; use the exercise anyway to pick themes)
- Education: don’t abbreviate course names; GPA optional
- Relevant Experience can include **current degree as a job** (“PhD student…”)
- “Not every resume needs to be one page, but the **first page** must prioritize this role”
- Skills last; list JD skills first; **no proficiency labels**; show the same skills in bullets; spoken languages OK

52-page guide (Jun 2026) extra:

- Pick **3–5 takeaways** before writing; résumé is a highlight reel
- Five pillars: keywords in **bullets not only Skills**; tailor every job; SCO; quantify (“trained **5** students each semester”, “collaborated with **2** engineering PhDs and an MBA”); simple format, PDF
- ATS: single column, no tables/graphics/odd fonts, standard **Education / Experience / Skills**; weave JD phrases everywhere; ATS “doesn’t care about color”
- AI: prompts to pull transferable skills from a CV, rewrite bullets, suggest verbs — **editor**, not author (same as Harvard/Yale). Anticipates ATS scoring soft skills via cover letter/LinkedIn
- Master résumé of everything, then cut (hub FAQ)
- Summary useful on **2–3 page** or pivot résumés; 4–5 themed lines, never “great education and experience”
- US: if a posting says “CV/Resume” for a non-faculty job, send a **résumé**

## 12.5 Master’s / PhD galleries (rules, not every file)

Master’s page: begin with **best qualifier** (Education while enrolled); order sections by the JD; bullets = you + action verb + 1–2 lines + context + transferable skill + result; **≥0.5" margins, ≥10 pt**; official degree name matching transcript; locations city/ST right-aligned; months on every date; ALL-CAPS headers miss spellcheck. Dates on the **right** so the eye hits title not calendar. Several versions per path beat one stuffed page. MPH sample is **2 pages** because of volume — they still suggest a Summary on that one.

PhD gallery FAQ: two pages common for research-heavy roles; **consulting = 1 page**; Selected Publications (n/N); enough technical detail without jargon unless the role is highly technical.

**Engineering PhD samples:** Bioengineering 2-pager (downloaded) and CBE **1-page consulting**. Use as academic→industry translation, not the candidate’s 1-page SWE stack. Consulting 1-pager is the length analog; still education-heavy.

## 12.6 ATS / AI blog (Brasch, Oct 2024)

1. Penn templates; **no headers/footers**; no graphics/symbols  
   **Tension with hub PhD rule** (page number in header/footer). For an experienced candidate 1-pager: **no header/footer content** (TIH/Yale/this blog).
2. Tailor with **exact JD phrases** (“leadership experience” not a list of club titles)
3. Workday-class ATS likes standard headers; don’t list “project management” without a bullet that **uses** it; expand acronyms once (`Customer Relationship Management (CRM)`)

Jobscan “98% of Fortune 500 use ATS” is a vendor stat — treat as “many large employers parse PDFs,” not a license to keyword-stuff (woodworksio: stuffing still dies in the human pass).

## 12.7 Cover letter (sibling channel)

- Why this org/role + which skills you bring; narrative, not a résumé dump
- Tailor; generic letters look uninterested
- 11–12 pt; **1 page**
- Don’t say “uniquely valuable contribution” — give evidence so they conclude it
- Avoid “perfect/best candidate,” unparalleled/overjoyed, overused **passion**
- Body: 2–3 JD skills with examples (academics/clubs/jobs)
- Recycle sections across similar jobs; if you name a contact, add **what you learned**
- 2-pager: address the org not required; `Dear Name` or “Hiring Manager”; letter is **why** (why experiences, why role, why org); energizing language OK in paragraphs
