## 17.1 Grissett — entry-level tech résumé

Four résumé headings on the primary page:

**Length / order.** Concise, direct, impactful. Typically **not much longer than one page**. Hiring managers skim many files — only **most recent and relevant**. Put the **most directly applicable** content **at the top**. If you lack related employment: classes, group projects, and **unrelated past jobs** still count as transferable skills.

**Live document / ATS.** Tailor **each** application. Steal **keywords from the JD**. “Not a ‘one and done’ deal” — updating from the posting helps the résumé surface in ATS.

**Format / file.** Clean, legible, professional font. **Skills + a summary of accomplishments at the beginning** (“curb appeal”). **Against premade resume templates** — she says they are often **flagged in ATS**. Save **PDF**. Filename: **your name + the position title**.

**Soft skills.** Time management, leadership, interpersonal — “always useful” for career changers; **include them** so any market can see them.

No bullet formula, no section list beyond skills/summary-first, no margin/font sizes, no GitHub, no sample layout.

## 17.2 Same-site résumé siblings

**7 Steps (Aug 2023).** Step 6: content **relevant to the job**; show **technical and soft** skills. Portfolio of past work if you have one. Step 4: certs “look good on your resume.” Rest is research → IT Support training → certs → network → apply. Follow-up thank-you after interviews.

**IT Expert (Oct 2024).** Marketing-long. Résumé paragraph is the same living-document / tailor line. Open source on GitHub as hands-on experience. Soft-skill list (adaptability, teamwork, time management) is career advice, not a skills-line spec. CompTIA A+ / CCNA / Azure / AWS / CISSP / PMP / ITIL / CEH — only list what you actually hold.

**ResumeBoost (Apr 2025).** Internal tool: AI draft from **coursework and performance**, then career coaches refine. They claim **6 hours → 30 minutes**. AdeptID ranks alumni résumés for employer partners. School-gated; **AI authors the first draft**.

**Collins (2018).** Tailor to JD/key requirements; network more than spray-apply; apply on company sites; LinkedIn free of typos; track companies / interviews / references.

**Matre (2019).** Professional email **with your name**; log every résumé send; follow up ~a week later. LinkedIn as a professional tool. Not layout advice.

## 17.3 Interview (job-search, not résumé generation)

Primary page is half interview. Short keep:

- Research the company **and** the interviewer on LinkedIn
- Practice common questions; don’t sound scripted
- Google yourself; social should match company values (or stay quiet)
- Interview = two-way conversation. Closing prompt they like: “is there anything I can disclose or speak to that shows my fit?”
- Ask questions that aren’t on the careers page (day-in-the-life, challenges, growth, culture, team goals)

Not used to write the 1-pager.
